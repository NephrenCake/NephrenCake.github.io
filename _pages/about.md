Hi, I am an LLM infrastructure researcher at RAI Studio, Xiaohongshu. I received my M.S. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I was a member of the Emerging Parallel Computing Center (EPCC) and was advised by [Professor Chen Chen](https://chenc10.github.io/). Previously, I worked on LLM inference optimization with the XDS Team at Huawei Cloud, where I was fortunate to work with and be mentored by [Yizhou Shan](http://lastweek.io/).

My research focuses on building efficient LLM inference systems. I have also worked on cluster scheduling and distributed caching.

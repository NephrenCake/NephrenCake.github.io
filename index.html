Yifei Liu

Researcher at RAI Studio, Xiaohongshu

Hi, I am an LLM infrastructure researcher at RAI Studio, Xiaohongshu. I received my M.S. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I was a member of the Emerging Parallel Computing Center (EPCC) and was advised by Professor Chen Chen. Previously, I worked on LLM inference optimization with the XDS Team at Huawei Cloud, where I was fortunate to work with and be mentored by Yizhou Shan.

My research focuses on building efficient LLM inference systems. I have also worked on cluster scheduling and distributed caching.

For more information, please explore this website or refer to my CV.
